Discovery of the Month
The Doorstone of the 'Phoenix' Tomb
Datec. 2300 BCE — Umm an-Nar period
MaterialLimestone, Jebel Haqlah
Dimensions126 × 73 × 20 cm
CollectionAl Ain National Museum
A masterful stone mason sculpted this doorstone from a sizeable limestone block quarried at Jebel Haqlah over 4,300 years ago, to be placed at the entrance of a monumental Umm an-Nar tomb. Over ten people were needed to lift it into its place.
